# Stephen Chow Style Mapping

## Core Style

- Absurd comedy with sincere emotional core
- Underdog rise through humiliation, persistence, and reversal
- Exaggerated behavior anchored by genuine feeling
- Fast comic rhythm that can suddenly turn touching

## Cross-Skill Impact

### Writer

Impact:
- Comedy should not stay at gag level only; it should build toward emotional payoff.
- The protagonist often begins from a low-status or ridiculous position.
- Supporting characters should be highly recognizable and capable of stealing scenes.

Guidance:
- Use escalation, misunderstanding, overconfidence, embarrassment, and reversal as engines.
- Let jokes reveal dignity, longing, or pain underneath the surface.
- Keep character goals simple and emotionally readable.

### Director

Impact:
- Performance rhythm should be expressive, sharp, and timing-driven.
- Reaction shots, sudden pauses, exaggerated close-ups, and comic contrast are important.
- Transitions can swing quickly between ridiculousness and sincerity.

Guidance:
- Favor punchy timing, hard reaction beats, comedic holds, and forceful visual reveals.
- Use close-up for facial expression, absurd props, and comic interruption points.

### Art

Impact:
- Props, costumes, and spaces should help the comedy read instantly.
- Environments can look heightened, playful, or slightly theatrical.
- Visual design should support status contrast: shabby vs grand, weak vs heroic, ridiculous vs epic.

Guidance:
- Favor memorable props, expressive silhouettes, comic costume contrast, and visually legible staging.
- Let the world amplify both the joke and the eventual emotional turn.

### Generation

Recommended prompt keywords:
- `absurd comedy energy`
- `expressive reaction shot`
- `underdog hero transformation`
- `playful exaggerated staging`
- `comic sincerity`

Avoid:
- Writing only `Stephen Chow style` without comic structure or emotional reversal
- Copying iconic scenes, lines, props, or choreography from specific films

## Narrative Traits

- underdog rise
- humiliation to breakthrough
- absurdity carrying hidden sincerity
- comedy that turns into emotional payoff

## Visual Traits

- exaggerated expressions
- absurd prop emphasis
- playful but readable staging
- contrast between low-status setup and heroic payoff

## Camera Traits

- expressive close-up
- reaction-shot rhythm
- sudden push or reveal for comic emphasis
- dynamic framing during comic-action escalation

## Dialogue Traits

- absurd logic delivered seriously
- fast exchanges and punchy interruptions
- catchphrase-like repetition with escalation
- jokes that can pivot into sincerity

## Character Traits

- underestimated protagonist
- unforgettable supporting roles
- foolish exterior with inner dignity
- rivals or authority figures with comic exaggeration

## Rhythm Traits

- fast comic beat
- escalating gag structure
- sudden emotional drop after laughter
- strong payoff at the end of a build-up

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not reduce the style to only noise, slapstick, or meme-like nonsense.
- Do not keep the absurd surface while losing the emotional sincerity underneath.
- Do not directly recreate specific scenes, punchlines, props, or action set pieces from existing films.
