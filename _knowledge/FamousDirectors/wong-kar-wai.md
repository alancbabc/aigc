# Wong Kar-wai Style Mapping

## Core Style

- Emotion over event
- Fragmented time and memory drift
- Urban loneliness and missed connection
- Desire expressed through restraint, distance, and residue

## Cross-Skill Impact

### Writer

Impact:
- Plot progression can be lighter, but emotional residue must stay strong.
- Relationship misalignment matters more than overt conflict.
- Memory, longing, waiting, and near-misses can carry the story engine.

Guidance:
- Do not explain everything early.
- Reuse motifs, repeated lines, and interior reflections to deepen feeling.
- Favor emotional continuation over mechanical exposition.

### Director

Impact:
- Camera should observe intimate emotional distance rather than only cover action.
- Blocking should use obstruction, reflection, narrow spaces, and pass-by movement.
- Transitions work best when they preserve mood, time residue, or visual memory.

Guidance:
- Prefer close-up, over-the-shoulder, partial obstruction, and reflective framing.
- Use pauses, lingering glances, and slow emotional movement.

### Art

Impact:
- Environments should support intimacy, confinement, memory, and urban solitude.
- Surfaces, windows, mirrors, hallways, rain, and night reflections become emotional carriers.
- Character styling should feel controlled, elegant, and slightly worn by emotion.

Guidance:
- Favor tight interiors, corridors, neon streets, rain, glass, mirror, and window frames.
- Use space itself to express relationship distance.

### Generation

Recommended prompt keywords:
- `melancholic neon atmosphere`
- `tight urban intimacy`
- `memory-like slow motion`
- `reflective surfaces`
- `lonely night city mood`

Avoid:
- Writing only `Wong Kar-wai style` without any specific emotional or visual traits
- Directly imitating iconic scenes, compositions, or signature moments from specific films

## Narrative Traits

- Emotion outweighs plot mechanics
- Stories often center on waiting, missing, remembering, and not quite arriving
- Time may feel felt rather than strictly linear
- Event count can stay small if emotional echo remains strong

## Visual Traits

- Neon night atmosphere
- Reflections and obstructed views
- Tight urban interiors
- Rain, smoke, windows, mirrors, and corridors

## Camera Traits

- close-up emphasis
- obstructed framing
- lingering looks
- slow-motion emotional passage

## Dialogue Traits

- ellipsis and silence
- reflective voice-over
- repeated lines with shifting emotion
- short speech with emotional aftertaste

## Character Traits

- emotionally restrained
- misaligned relationships
- urban loneliness
- calm exterior with strong internal movement

## Rhythm Traits

- slow
- suspended
- pauses with emotional residue
- no rush to close every dramatic beat

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not reduce the style to only filters, slow motion, or neon color.
- Do not keep the surface mood while losing the underlying emotional misalignment.
- Do not directly recreate specific scenes, monologues, or iconic compositions from existing films.
