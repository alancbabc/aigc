# Tsui Hark Style Mapping

## Core Style

- High kinetic momentum
- Strong visual impact
- Romanticized jianghu tension
- Fantastical action imagination

## Cross-Skill Impact

### Writer

Impact:
- Conflict should appear more externally and escalate faster.
- Character relationships should carry jianghu pressure, loyalty, rivalry, and fate.
- Heroic and legendary energy can be stronger than everyday realism.

Guidance:
- Prioritize shifts in power, danger, and momentum over quiet interior rumination.
- Let entrances, reveals, and reversals carry force and identity.

### Director

Impact:
- Camera should feel more dynamic, forceful, and movement-driven.
- Action staging should emphasize pressure, rhythm, entry vectors, and spatial attack lines.
- Transitions can be sharper and more decisive when energy needs to carry forward.

Guidance:
- Use stronger angle changes, motion pressure, and impact-based staging.
- Favor directional force, leaps, rushes, clashes, and sudden compositional shifts.

### Art

Impact:
- Environments should feel legendary, heightened, and dramatic rather than merely realistic.
- Character silhouettes should stay bold and recognizable.
- Space should support jianghu scale, danger, and theatrical motion.

Guidance:
- Favor inns, mountain paths, sect compounds, rain, bamboo groves, temples, cliffs, and martial landmarks.
- Push red, gold, deep shadow, wind, fabric motion, and mythic texture.

### Generation

Recommended prompt keywords:
- `dynamic wuxia energy`
- `fantastical action framing`
- `heroic martial spectacle`
- `bold color contrast`

Avoid:
- Writing only `Tsui Hark style` without concrete action or visual traits
- Directly copying iconic choreography or signature sequences from specific films

## Narrative Traits

- jianghu and power tension
- romance mixed with danger and fate
- faster conflict escalation
- legendary tone over slice-of-life realism

## Visual Traits

- bold color contrast
- fantastical movement
- dramatic martial space
- visual pressure before explanation

## Camera Traits

- fast motion emphasis
- strong angle changes
- spatial pressure within combat or confrontation
- high-energy composition

## Dialogue Traits

- direct exchanges under pressure
- theatrical tension
- heroic declaration when needed
- sharp emotional turns in conflict scenes

## Character Traits

- heroic presence
- larger-than-life silhouettes
- eccentric supporting roles
- rivals and allies with strong identity signatures

## Rhythm Traits

- fast escalation
- dense conflict beats
- alternating action burst and emotional release

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not reduce the style to only retro wuxia filters or costume nostalgia.
- Do not keep only action intensity while losing jianghu relationships and mythic tension.
- Do not directly recreate specific scenes, fights, or iconic choreography from existing films.
